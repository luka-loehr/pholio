Tabs and accordions

Tabs with a default, grouped tabs that stay in sync, and single or multiple accordions.

Tabs

items lists the labels, separated by |. Each Tab takes its value from the matching label. With updateAnchor, choosing a tab also updates the URL hash, so the link you copy opens that tab.

Archives live in ~/Library/Application Support/Lanternfly.

Choosing the default

Pay once a year and get two months free.

Grouped tabs

Tabs with the same groupId switch together. With persist, the choice is remembered across pages and visits.

A label that itself contains a pipe is escaped: items="a\|b|c" yields the two tabs "a|b" and "c".

Multiple
