How to: open a support ticket
=============================

Updated: September 2026
Audience: viewers and editors


1. Sign in
----------

Open https://example.com/ and sign in with your
account. If you belong to several teams, pick the
right team in the top right corner.


2. Fill in the ticket
---------------------

Click “Tickets” in the menu and fill in the form:

  Field           Required  Example
  --------------  --------  ------------------------------
  Date from       yes       15.09.2026
  Date to         yes       16.09.2026
  Reason          no        Broken export
  Note            no        Call back after 10:30

Important: tickets must arrive by the second working day (48 hours).
After that they are closed automatically.


3. Confirmation
---------------

After sending you see the message:

    Saved: ticket #1234

You also receive an email. The team lead is informed
automatically.


4. Common errors
----------------

  * "Date to is before date from" – dates swapped.
  * "Name must not be empty" – no team selected.
  * Error 422 – form incomplete; please check the required fields.
  * Error 500 – please try again later or write to support@example.com
    instead.


5. Attach a file
----------------

From the third day a log file is required. Upload it as
PDF or photo (JPG, PNG, max. 10 MB) under “Documents”.

> Tip: photograph the screen on a dark background
> so the text is easy to read.
