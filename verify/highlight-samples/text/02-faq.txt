Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

Q: I forgot my password. What now?
A: Click “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page. Within a few
   minutes you receive an email with a link. The link is valid
   for 24 hours.

Q: Why can't I see the billing page?
A: The billing page is only visible to admins. Check that
   your account belongs to a team.

Q: Can I open a ticket by phone?
A: Yes, at +33 1 23 45 67 89 (front desk, Mon–Fri 7:30–13:00).

Q: What does "C" mean on the release board?
A: C = cancelled, R = release, M = moved.
